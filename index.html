I am a PhD student at the Department of Mathematics of the Universität Hamburg, supervised by Ingo Runkel and Volker Schomerus.

My position is funded by the Collaborative Research Center 1624: Higher Structures, Moduli Spaces and Integrability (Project A.4).

I am looking for a postdoc beginning in Autumn/Winter of 2027!

I am interested in algebraic structures that arise in integrability, and conformal and topological field theories. The main goal of my thesis is to further develop the theory of skein modules with braided module categories. Such skein modules require so-called braided module categories as input, and I am interested in producing (re)construction results for these gadgets.

I also like to think about topological symmetries, in particular their realisation in quantum and statistical lattice models.
