I am a PhD student at the Department of Mathematics supervised by Prof. Dr. Ingo Runkel. My position is funded by the Collaborative Research Center 1624: Higher Structures, Moduli Spaces and Integrability (Project A.4).

I am interested in algebraic structures that arise in conformal, integral, and topological field theories. The main goal of my thesis is to develop further the theory of skein modules with line defects, and apply it to study the relations between skein theory and integrable systems. Such skein modules require so-called braided module categories as input, and I am interested in producing (re)construction results for these gadgets.

I also like to think about topological symmetries, in particular their realisation in quantum and statistical lattice models.
